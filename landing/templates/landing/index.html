About the System

Structured financial aid built for clarity and accountability.

Traditional scholarship workflows suffer from fragmented paperwork, opaque review timelines, and mismatched donor funds. ScholarBridge organizes every stage into dedicated portals for administrators, students, and sponsors.

For Students

Discover eligible scholarships, reuse verified transcripts and income certificates, and monitor every review milestone from submission to award.

For Donors & Foundations

Specify target districts, minimum CGPA, and household income thresholds while tracking exact fund utilization and supported scholars.

For Administrators

Audit applications, validate supporting documents, and review data-driven eligibility recommendations in a single governance workspace.

Platform Capabilities

Everything required to administer merit and need-based awards.

Each module operates independently with dedicated controls while sharing consistent eligibility standards.

Scholarship Management

Publish structured scholarship programs with clear award amounts, CGPA floors, income limits, and application deadlines.

Student Applications

Guided application forms that surface academic history, household income details, and real-time review timelines.

Donor Funding

Allocate grants with city, district, and degree preferences while monitoring remaining balances and student headcount.

Document Verification

Side-by-side previewing for CNIC records, transcripts, and income statements with clear verified or rejected states.

Intelligent Scholarship Matching

ML-assisted eligibility scoring ranks candidate-to-fund fit with transparent reasoning for review committees.
