Full Program Description

The STEM Excellence Award supports full-time undergraduate students enrolled in Computer Science, Software Engineering, Electrical Engineering, and Applied Mathematics across accredited partner universities. Awards cover annual tuition, laboratory consumables, and textbook allowances for two consecutive semesters.

Eligibility Requirements

  • Enrolled in an accredited 4-year undergraduate STEM degree program.
  • Minimum cumulative GPA of 3.50 on a 4.00 scale verified by official registrar transcript.
  • Monthly household income must not exceed $650 with valid supporting income certificate.
  • No concurrent full-tuition scholarship from external institutional sponsors.
